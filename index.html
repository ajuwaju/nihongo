/06the fast route, in short

  1. kana first, in a week. hiragana then katakana until reading them is automatic. the about page has the tables.
  2. every day, three small things. clear the kanji and vocab reviews (ten minutes), open one grammar point and do its drill, read one article or passage out loud.
  3. new cards, fifteen a day. that pace alone covers n5 kanji in a week and n4 in a month. raise it when reviews feel light.
  4. listen before you look. in listening tests play the audio twice, answer, then reveal the script. dictate one line a day.
  5. mock exam every sunday. quizzes → mock exam, at your target level. a steady 80% means you are ready to move up.
  6. write it down. anything that trips you twice becomes a note in the drawer. read the drawer on the train.
